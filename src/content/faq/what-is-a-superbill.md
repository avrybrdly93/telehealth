---
question: What is a superbill and how do I get one?
group: Costs & superbills
order: 7
---

A superbill is an itemized receipt of your visit. You can send it to your insurance company to
ask for reimbursement. We provide one after each appointment. We can't guarantee your plan will
reimburse you, so check with your insurer about your out-of-network benefits.
