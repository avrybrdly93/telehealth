This page will give a plain-language overview of what to expect when consenting to
telehealth care — for example, how video visits work, their benefits and limitations
compared to in-person care, privacy considerations, and what to do if the connection is
lost or in a mental health emergency. Any binding consent a patient gives happens through
the scheduling/video vendor's own flow, not this page.

No telehealth consent overview text has been drafted yet. Nothing on this page should be
treated as the actual consent a patient gives before a visit.
