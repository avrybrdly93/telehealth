# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

Be the psychiatry practice a Californian would recommend to their own family: easy to understand, easy to reach, clinically excellent, and respectful of the courage it takes to seek mental health care.

## The Problem We Solve

Seeking psychiatric care today typically means: confusing directories, weeks-long waits, unclear pricing, clinical jargon, and websites that feel either sterile or salesy. For someone already struggling, each point of friction is a reason to give up.

## Our Product Answer (MVP)

A website that does five things exceptionally well:
1. **Explains** what psychiatric services we offer, for whom, in plain language.
2. **Introduces** the two providers (MD psychiatrist, PMHNP) with real credentials, real photos, real voice.
3. **Prices** services transparently — self-pay rates on a public page, no "call for pricing".
4. **Books** a first appointment in a short, mobile-first scheduling flow.
5. **Reassures** — calm design, crisis resources, clear "what happens next" at every step.

## Experience Pillars

| Pillar | Meaning in practice |
|---|---|
| Professional | Real credentials, correct terminology, no stock-photo clichés |
| Human | First-person provider voice, warm copy, photos of the actual clinicians |
| Calm | Generous whitespace, muted palette, no motion that demands attention |
| Safe | Crisis resources everywhere, honest scope-of-care statements, privacy clarity |
| Modern | Fast, mobile-first, accessible, no dated healthcare-portal aesthetics |
| Clinically trustworthy | Educational content reviewed by providers; no exaggerated outcome claims |

## What We Are Not
- Not a therapy-matching marketplace.
- Not an urgent/crisis care service (and we say so explicitly, with alternatives).
- Not an insurance-first practice (yet — see roadmap in ../06_PROJECT/ROADMAP.md).

## North Star Metric

**Qualified booked consultations per week** (California adults booking an intake), balanced by show rate and patient-reported ease of booking. Details in ../08_OPERATIONS/ANALYTICS_PLAN.md.
