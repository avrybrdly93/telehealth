# Copy Guidelines

## Voice
Warm clinician, plain speaker. We sound like a good doctor explaining things across a table — not a brand, not a brochure, not a startup.

## Hard Rules (blocking per QUALITY_STANDARD.md)

1. **No outcome guarantees.** Banned: "get better", "feel like yourself again", "proven results", "life-changing". Allowed: "evidence-based care", "we'll build a plan together".
2. **No medical advice.** Educational content describes conditions and typical treatment approaches generally; it never tells the reader what they have or should take. Condition pages include: "This page is educational and isn't a diagnosis. A psychiatric evaluation is how we understand your specific situation."
3. **No urgency/scarcity language.** Banned: "limited spots", "act now", "don't wait" (exception: crisis copy may say "call 988 now").
4. **Person-first, non-stigmatizing language.** "person living with bipolar disorder" not "a bipolar"; "died by suicide" not "committed suicide"; never "crazy/insane" even colloquially.
5. **Accurate titles.** MD is "psychiatrist"; PMHNP is "psychiatric nurse practitioner" — never blur the two ("our doctors" is banned when referring to both).
6. **Crisis copy is fixed text.** The 988/911 block wording is defined once in the component library and never paraphrased per-page.

## Style Rules
- Reading level ≤ 8th grade for patient-facing pages (UX-002; checked in CI). Legal pages exempt.
- Sentences ≤ 20 words on average. One idea per paragraph. Front-load the answer.
- Second person ("you"), active voice, contractions welcome.
- Numbers as numerals ("60-minute evaluation"). Prices always with $ and no asterisks.
- Headings are informative, not clever: "What a first appointment costs", not "Investment in yourself".

## Microcopy Standards
- Buttons: verb-first, specific. "Book an appointment", "See pricing", "Continue to scheduling". Never "Submit", "Learn more", "Click here".
- Form labels always visible (no placeholder-as-label). Error copy pattern in ERROR_STATES.md.
- Links describe destination: "our cancellation policy", not "here".

## Terminology Glossary (use exactly these)
appointment (not "session/slot" in patient copy) · video visit (not "telemedicine call") · psychiatric evaluation (not "assessment/consult") · follow-up appointment (not "med check") · self-pay (not "cash pay") · superbill (define on first use per page).
